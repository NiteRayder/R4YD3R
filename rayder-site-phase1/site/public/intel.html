Intel

Frequently asked questions, declassified.

What is Rayder?

Brook's (NiteRayder's) own home base — posts, releases, social feeds, and member-only content in one place that no platform can bury or take away.

What's the difference between Super Rayder and VIR?

Super Rayder ($1/month) unlocks the exclusive raw footage and behind-the-scenes content in The Deep Vault, exclusive Discord channels, an end-of-video shoutout, and entry into the Friday gifted-sub draws. VIR ($5/month) gets all of that plus potential team membership, guaranteed content requests, a start-of-video shoutout, and a personal DM/shoutout.

What stays free?

Transmissions (all content posts), Signals, and everything on the public channels. Posts are never sub-locked — memberships gate the members area and The Deep Vault, not the writing.

How do payments work?

Memberships are paid through PayPal — you subscribe on PayPal's own pages, and PayPal tells this site when your membership is active. The site never sees or stores your card or account details.

How do I cancel?

Inside your own PayPal account, anytime — your membership here follows your PayPal subscription automatically. No emails, no guilt trips, no dark patterns.

How do the Friday gifted subs work?

They're funded from the combined earnings of all members. Three gifts go out each week, and the winners are chosen on Fridays.

When do memberships open?

The site is being built now and memberships switch on at launch. Watch Transmissions and the socials for the date.
